# USS Voyager — Defensive, Tactical & Sensor Systems (Canon Research)

Scope: Memory Alpha (MA) canon. Every claim carries a source URL. `[uncertain]` = explicitly marked. "not stated" = the source does not say.
Voyager is an *Intrepid*-class starship: 15 decks, ~700,000 t, crew 141 at launch (VOY "Caretaker"), ~150 in service; 14 external shield grids; 14 phaser arrays. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_class; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Voyager)

## 1. Deflector shields
- **Function:** force-field of energetic distortion with high graviton concentration around the hull. Divided into six sections: forward, starboard, port, aft, dorsal, ventral. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Deflector_shield)
- **Canon numbers:** Intrepid-class has **14 external shield grids**; shields include multiphasic and multi-spectrum shielding; survive a binary pulsar for minutes and a planetary explosion at short range. (Intrepid_class)
- **Limits/failure:** continuous/powerful discharges erode integrity to failure; reported as % ("holding"/"buckling"/"failing"); solar flares weaken them; phased polaron beams bypass them entirely (USS *Odyssey*). (Deflector_shield)
- **Hosts:** hull-embedded grid emitters; no single compartment. Voyager can transport through its own shields (later-era design). (Deflector_shield)
- **Failure looks like:** rising % loss per section → local penetration → hull hits. (Deflector_shield)
- **Evidence:** VOY "Equinox, Part II" (14 grids), "Flashback"/"The Omega Directive" (multiphasic/multi-spectrum), "Scientific Method", "Think Tank".
- **Model:** per-section (6) integrity %, regen rate, frequency/harmonics, emitter power draw, bypass conditions.

## 2. Navigational deflector
- **Function:** dish-shaped force-beam generator emitting low-power shields (microparticles) and high-power deflector/tractor beams (larger debris). (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Navigational_deflector)
- **Locations (Intrepid):** main deflector at the extreme forward of **Decks 10–13**; secondary deflector at forward of **Deck 6**. Deflector Control is a compartment (VOY "Year of Hell, Part II" fire here). (Navigational_deflector)
- **Limits/failure:** over-channelling burns out the dish (repairs/replacement); VGR deflector lost in "Year of Hell" → unprotected micrometeoroid damage; "Shattered" used as a lightning rod and burned out.
- **Note:** MA stresses defensive shields are "distinctly different from the navigational deflector." (Navigational_deflector)
- **Model:** deflector online/offline + burn-out state; separate from shields.

## 3. Hull composition
- **Tritanium:** pure = 21.4× harder than diamond; Intrepid uses tritanium bulkheads; used since ENT era. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Tritanium)
- **Duranium:** strong metallic alloy; **Voyager's hull is identified as duranium** (Borg drone "One", VOY "Drone"). Spare duranium sheeting stored in Cargo Bay 1. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Duranium)
- TNG *Technical Manual* (background, `[uncertain]`) describes an AGP ablative ceramic outer layer over a 0.15 cm tritanium foil. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Ablative_armor)
- **Model:** hull integrity per-deck; material hardness as damage-resistance coefficient.

## 4. Structural integrity field (SIF)
- **Function:** force field supplementing hull structure against warp/spatial stress; strength measured in %. 100% = all load carried by field. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Structural_integrity_field)
- **Limits/failure:** as % drops, hull takes stress directly; if the SIF around the warp coils collapses, **coils rupture within a short time** (VOY "One"). SIF down + warp → **outer hull plates torn off** (VOY "Year of Hell", alt timeline). DS9 SIF failure tore bulkheads.
- **Model:** SIF % (0–100), power allocation, coupling to warp-coil and hull-integrity state.

## 5. Tractor beam
- **Function:** attenuated linear graviton beam to hold/move/repel objects. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Tractor_beam)
- **Locations (Intrepid):** forward emitter under the main deflector dish; second emitter at the aft end of the secondary hull, **aft section of Deck 14**. (Intrepid_class)
- **Limits/failure:** normally sub-warp only; warp tow needs matched velocities or shearing; magnetic-field interference blocks lock-on.
- **Model:** emitter online/offline; target mass; lock success; power draw.

## 6. Phaser arrays & banks
- **Function:** directed-energy nadion particle beams; effects range by intensity/field: **stun → kill → vaporize/disintegrate** (hand phasers given vaporize/disintegrate ~2262). (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Phaser; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Beam_setting)
- **Intrepid numbers:** 14 phaser arrays (dorsal/ventral saucer, ventral engineering, pylon undersides, shuttlebay); plus ≥4 phaser banks: two in the aft torpedo launchers, one aft bank behind the second tractor emitter (**Deck 14**), one embedded emitter on the right nacelle. Model shows 13 arrays; large strips are type-10 (same as Galaxy). `[uncertain]` background. (Intrepid_class; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Phaser_array)
- **Stun setting:** non-lethal/disorienting; some species immune. (Beam_setting)
- **Limits/failure:** array/physics can be disabled (Vidiian hyper-thermic charge, VOY "Deadlock"); Borg adaptation (see §12).
- **Model:** per-array health, power setting (stun→vaporize), charge/heat, arc coverage.

## 7. Photon torpedoes
- **Function:** warp-capable matter/antimatter warheads. Voyager carries **Type 6** (spec: 210 cm; 247.5 kg unloaded; max 1.5 kg antimatter warhead; blast zone 300 km; effective range ~8,000,000 km; 25 isotons; class-6 warhead = 200 isotons). Also class-9 and ≥4 class-10 (high-yield) and 2 tricobalt devices. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Type_6_photon_torpedo; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Photon_torpedo; Intrepid_class)
- **Launchers:** four standard tubes (two fore, two aft); forward launchers sit just above the main deflector; aft launchers double as phaser banks. (Intrepid_class; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Torpedo_launcher)
- **Complement:** MA gives no hard total for 2371 Voyager — **not stated**. Episode inventory shown only for nanoprobe torpedoes: 13 standard +1 class-10 ("Scorpion, Part II"), later 19 standard +3 class-10 ("In the Flesh"). (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Class_10_photon_torpedo)
- **Limits:** finite inventory, no subspace tearing; Borg adapt to transphasic torpedoes after several shots ("Endgame").
- **Model:** torpedo count by type, tube load/cooldown, fore/aft arcs.

## 8. Ablative armour generator (Endgame)
- **Function:** shield emitters project a nearly-impenetrable ablative armour layer over the hull. Acquired 2378 from alt-timeline Admiral Janeway. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Ablative_generator)
- **Limits/failure:** armour integrity eventually fails under sustained Borg pursuit ("Endgame"); not standard by 2404; Borg Queen assimilated the design.
- **Model:** armour deployed yes/no; armour integrity %; activation/deployment animation state; limited duration.

## 9. Point defence
- **Status:** MA has no "point defense" starship-system page for Voyager — **not stated**. Closest canon systems: automatic bridge defence system (TAS) and tractor/anti-missile use of phasers. Do not invent a dedicated PD grid.
- **Model:** optional — treat as rapid-fire phaser mode, not a separate system.

## 10. Sensors & astrometrics
- **Long-range:** scans wide areas for ships/objects. Enterprise-D: 10 ly radius/24 h (≈ one sector/day); TOS: 1 parsec near-instant. Voyager not given a number — `[uncertain]`. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Long_range_sensor_scan)
- **Short-range:** close-proximity objects; no range number (VOY uses it repeatedly: "Caretaker", "The Cloud"). (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Short_range_scan)
- **Sensor arrays:** forward + lateral arrays; tactical sensors tied to weapons. Intrepid boasted the **best navigational sensors** in Starfleet at launch. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Sensor_array; Intrepid_class)
- **Astrometrics:** lab built by Kim & Seven with Borg tech; measures radiative flux of **3 billion stars** at once, 10× more accurate; wraparound holographic wall screen; wholly disabled → navigation/route plotting degraded. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Astrometrics)
- **Model:** long/short scan modes, range slider, resolution + interference, sensor-array health, astrometrics as a data-quality modifier.

## 11. Communications
- **Subspace radio:** speed varies by tech (~52,000 c ≈ 144.5 ly/day cited). (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Subspace_communication)
- **Voyager-specific:** 2374 used ancient relay stations → near-instant Delta↔Alpha ("Message in a Bottle"); via Pathfinder, 2374–2378 monthly transmissions → eventually two-way live visual at **30,000 ly, 11 min/day**. (USS_Voyager)
- **Model:** comm-link status (none / relay / two-way), latency, data-rate; relay-station reachability.

## 12. Borg adaptation (mechanics)
- Borg shields nullify nearly any energy weapon and their weapons penetrate nearly any shield, usually **within minutes**. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Borg)
- **Phaser adapter** (2367): auto-re-tunes phasers to a random setting after each shot; rotating modulation lets **at most 12 shots** penetrate Borg shielding before adaptation. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Phaser_adapter)
- In "Dark Frontier" the Borg **triaxilated deflector-shield geometry** to absorb modulating phaser pulses. (Phaser_adapter)
- Drones have personal shields that adapt to energy weapons; adaptations propagate to the Collective. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Borg_drone)
- Against multi-adaptive shielding the Borg locked onto shield modulators, adapting "mere moments" after remodulation. (https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Shield_modulator)
- **Model:** per-weapon adaptation counter (shots before Borg immunity), modulation/rotation mechanic, Collective sharing of adaptations, time-to-adapt clock. This is the core Borg-boarding tension loop.

## Cross-cutting simulation note
Minimum global state: per-deck hull integrity + breach flags; 6-section shield % + featured 14 grids; SIF %; power budget feeding shields/weapons/SIF; weapon inventories (arrows/torpedoes) + cooldowns; sensor/comm mode; Borg adaptation counters. Failure cascades: shields down → hull/SIF load; SIF down + warp → plate loss; deflector loss → micrometeoroid damage.
