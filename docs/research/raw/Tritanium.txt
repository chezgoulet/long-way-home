'''Tritanium''' was an [[ore]] known to be, when [[purity|pure]], 21.4 times as [[hardness|hard]] as [[diamond]]. It was found, among other places, on the [[planet]] [[Argus X]]. ({{TOS|Obsession}}) The ability to melt tritanium was beyond [[Federation]] [[weapon|weapons]] [[technology]] in [[2364]]. ({{TNG|The Arsenal of Freedom}}) 

During the [[autopsy]] of [[Hon-Tihl]], the [[Klingon]] [[first officer]] of the {{IKS|Toh'Kaht}}, in [[2369]], [[Doctor]] [[Julian Bashir|Bashir]] found tritanium [[alloy]] fragments in his chest and upper arms, but determined that they were not the cause of his [[death]]. ({{DS9|Dramatis Personae}})

{{bginfo|According to the ''[[Star Fleet Medical Reference Manual]]'' (p. 19), the element tritanium, symbol Tn, atomic weight 323, atomic number 125, was discovered by the Federation on Argus X in [[2161]].}}

== Uses of tritanium ==
Tritanium [[alloy]] was a widely-used construction material. The [[bulkhead]]s of {{NX|Enterprise}} were composed of tritanium. ({{ENT|Dead Stop}}) It continued to be used in the [[23rd century]], when it was used in the {{class|Crossfield}}. ({{DIS|Saints of Imperfection}}) In the [[24th century]], [[Federation]] [[starship]]s, specifically {{class|Galaxy}}, {{class|Intrepid}}, and {{type|Raven}}, also had tritanium bulkheads. ({{TNG|Where Silence Has Lease}}; {{VOY|Year of Hell, Part II|The Raven|Repentance}})

The ''[[Erstwhile]]'', a [[Class 9 cargo vessel]], had a tritanium hull. ({{TNG|The Outrageous Okona}})

The [[hull]]s of [[Axanar cargo ship]]s in the [[2150s]] were composed of tritanium and [[disilicon polymer]]s. ({{ENT|Fight or Flight}})

{{bginfo|In the script for "Fight or Flight", the hull of an Axanar cargo vessel is instead referred to as containing [[ditanium]], although this is changed to tritanium in the final version of the episode.}}

The hulls of Federation [[Type 6 shuttlecraft]] were composed of tritanium. A [[Class 2 shuttle]]'s hull was also made from a tritanium alloy. ({{TNG|Rascals}}; {{VOY|Threshold}}) 

Tritanium was also used as a type of armor plating in [[skimmer|assault skimmers]]. ({{DS9|Business as Usual}})

Federation [[EJ7 interlock]]s were composed of a tritanium compound. In [[2369]] [[Chief]] [[Miles O'Brien|O'Brien]]'s went missing and he found the remains in a [[power conduit]] alongside the organic remains of [[Ensign]] [[Aquino]]. Prior to this find, the [[computer]] located a tritanium source in the [[tool locker]] in [[Deep Space 9 levels#Level three|level three]]. ({{DS9|In the Hands of the Prophets}}) 

The projectile fired from the [[TR-116 rifle]] was composed of tritanium. ({{DS9|Field of Fire}})

[[Borg]] [[tactical drone]]s possessed a tritanium infrastructure, as did [[Borg cube]]s. ({{VOY|Dark Frontier|Endgame}})

The detonator of a warhead in a firing range {{USS|Voyager}} accidentally wandered into was protected by tritanium shielding. ({{VOY|Human Error}})
