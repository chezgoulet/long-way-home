'''Regenerative shielding''' was an advanced form of [[deflector]] [[Deflector shield|shielding]] technology used  in the [[24th century]] by the [[Numiri]] culture of the [[Delta Quadrant]], and later developed independently by [[Starfleet]].

In [[2371]], when the {{USS|Voyager}} was engaged in combat with two [[Numiri patrol vessel]]s, [[Commander]] [[Chakotay]] utilized an old [[Maquis]] maneuver, wherein ''Voyager'' feigned distress to lure the enemy vessels into close proximity. When one of the patrol vessels attempted to lock a [[tractor beam]] onto ''Voyager'', Chakotay ordered [[phaser]]s fired at the vessels' [[navigational deflector]]s, successfully penetrating their regenerative shields and disabling both ships. ({{VOY|Ex Post Facto}})

The experimental vessel {{USS|Prometheus|Prometheus class}}, developed by Starfleet in [[2374]], was equipped with regenerative shielding, [[ablative hull armor]], and [[multi-vector assault mode]] as part of its primary battle systems. In that year, the regenerative shielding was utilized in battle with a group of [[Romulan]] [[Warbird|Warbirds]]. ({{VOY|Message in a Bottle}})
