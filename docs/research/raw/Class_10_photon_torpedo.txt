The '''class 10 photon torpedo''' was a type of [[photon torpedo]] used by {{Class|Intrepid}} [[starship]]s. 

Prior to engaging [[Species 8472]] in [[2374]], with the assistance of the [[Borg]], the crew of {{USS|Voyager}} created several [[bio-molecular weapon]]s, augmented with [[nanoprobe]]s, including thirteen standard photon torpedoes and one class 10, armed with a [[high yield warhead]]. ({{VOY|Scorpion, Part II}})

Later that year, when ''Voyager'' encountered [[Terrasphere 8]], [[Seven of Nine]] made Borg modifications to increase the ship's complement of nanoprobe enhanced torpedoes to include nineteen standard photon torpedoes and three class 10s armed with high-yield warheads. Upon learning this, [[Captain]] [[Kathryn Janeway]] wanted more modified torpedoes, but Seven warned her that they had depleted the supply of nanoprobes. Janeway urged her to find a way to make more, stating, "''I don't intend to run out of firepower mid-battle.''" ({{VOY|In the Flesh}})
