# USS Voyager — Interior, Life Support & Crew Systems (canon notes)

Sources: Memory Alpha (memory-alpha.fandom.com). Every claim carries its source URL. `not stated` = canon silent; **[uncertain]** flags contradiction.

## 2. Life support / environmental control
Atmosphere/heat/gravity maintenance; node = **Environmental control, D12** (deck list). Memory Alpha's "Life support" page covers only *medical* life support — starship environmental specifics `not stated` there. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Life_support

## 3. Artificial gravity
Generators in **floor gravity plating**; corridor/quarter levels adjustable; shuttlebays are variable-gravity areas. **Failure modes:** Voyager lost gravity on D8 (VOY "Prototype"), D11 (VOY "Prey"), D9 (VOY "The Haunting of Deck Twelve"); inversion possible; automatic bypass is a standard safety. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_gravity

## 4. Inertial dampers
Counter rapid acceleration; **warp impossible without them** (crew crushed, VOY "Tattoo"). Voyager: set to maximum to land, matched to planetary gravity once landed, "flight configuration" to depart (VOY "The 37's"); manual enable/disable; reset via base "three-zero-zero" then re-init (VOY "The Cloud"). Failures on Voyager (VOY "Equinox, Part II"); misalignment caused space-sickness (VOY "Relativity"). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Inertial_damping_system

## 5. Transporters
Three facilities on **D4** (rooms 1 & 2 operational); **six standard pads**; elevated control booth; bio-neural gel packs in processors; **emergency site-to-site directly to sickbay from anywhere in range, incl. inside the ship** (VOY "Shattered"). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_class
- **Range:** 24th-c. standard max ≈ **40,000 km**; early Earth ≈10,000 km. **Emergency transporters ≤10 km** (VOY "Future's End"). Reduced case: with computer components missing, Voyager needed to be **within 500 km** (VOY "Concerning Flight"). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Transporter
- **Pattern buffer:** stores matter stream after dematerialization; sits directly beneath the pad; microcellular scan per use; multiplex buffers eliminated transporter psychosis. **Once a pattern is in the buffer, transport can no longer be blocked** (VOY "Prototype"). Voyager effectively had **one buffer** per backstage reading **[uncertain]**. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Pattern_buffer
- **Biofilter:** screens known molecular patterns/pathogens; reprogrammable; evaluated by a **Level 4 diagnostic**. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Biofilter
- **Blocks:** active deflector shields (unless an EM window via rotating frequencies — exact timing + computer help); magnetic shields; dampening fields; ionic interference (ion storms); trinimbic interference; minerals e.g. kelbonite; annular-confinement-beam scattering; sensor-lock loss. Two transporters bridged by **system interlock** to beam through ionic interference. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Transporter
- Transporter rooms manned by a **transporter chief** (post lost in the initial crossing). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Transporter_room

## 6. Replicators & rationing
Matter-energy synthesis from replicator reserves; also recycling; food/drink variants; produces food, medication, clothing, components. Mess hall (D2, section 13) had **four food replicators**; captain's dining room reconfigured as a kitchen by Neelix. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Replicator ; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_class
- **Rationing:** energy-conservation policy used when power was limited, dropped when reserves recovered; never clearly enforced on-screen. A ration = a quantity of *energy*, not one use — a clarinet cost a **week's rations**, a locket **two weeks'**. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Replicator_ration
- **Limit:** replicators depend on the **forward gel-pack grid**; isolating it loses replicator ops while primary systems reroute (VOY "Learning Curve"). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Bio-neural_gel_pack

## 7. Holodecks
Voyager has **at least two**. Intrepid article: both on **Deck 6**, able to "impersonate matter even at the molecular level" (VOY "Phage"); deck list also cites a holodeck on D14 (VOY "Darkling") → **[uncertain]**. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Holodeck
- **Separate power:** run on **holodeck reactors**, independent of the main grid (safety feature, 2261). Voyager's holodeck energy matrix was **incompatible** with primary power; tapping it **blew half the ship's relays** (VOY "Parallax"). Holodecks are one of Voyager's **three independent power sources** (with shuttlecraft and life support). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Holodeck_reactor ; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Voyager

## 8. Sickbay & EMH (D5)
ICU, bio-hazard support, critical care, morgue, CMO's office, small medical lab; **three standard biobeds + one surgical bed**; **surgical bay** isolatable by force field; design shared with Sovereign class. Biobeds: biofunction monitors + **restraining force fields**; surgical bed has a ceiling sensor cluster. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_class ; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Biobed
- **EMH (Mark I):** "EMH Program AK-1 Diagnostic and Surgical Subroutine Omega-323", by Dr. Lewis Zimmerman; short-term emergency replacement for the CMO. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Emergency_Medical_Holographic_program
- **Mobile emitter:** 29th-century, palm-sized, poly-deutonic alloy; self-contained projector (photons + force fields); acquired VOY "Future's End, Part II"; let The Doctor leave sickbay/holodecks; ran ~a year in the "Year of Hell" timeline without charging concerns. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Mobile_emitter
- **Medical replicator:** no dedicated Memory Alpha page — `not stated` as a distinct system; replicators produce medication (VOY "Latent Image", "Fury", "Body and Soul"). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Replicator

## 9. Computer & bio-neural gel packs
Main computer accesses **47 million data channels**, sustains **575 trillion calcs/nanosecond**, 10–1,790 K. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_class
Gel packs: hybrid organic-electronic nodes running **half of critical systems**; **cannot be replicated**; behind access panels ship-wide (bridge, under transporter platform, holodeck, corridors/Jefferies tubes). **Failure modes:** bacterial/macroviral infection (VOY "Learning Curve", "Macrocosm"); subnucleonic radiation degraded **33% of relays** (VOY "One"); burnt out by EM discharge (VOY "Timeless"). **47 spare packs** aboard (2371). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Bio-neural_gel_pack

## 10. Turbolifts, Jefferies tubes, force fields, airlocks, escape pods, cargo
- **Turbolift:** vertical + horizontal via turboshafts, voice/text command; unusable without power (VOY "Macrocosm"). Voyager: turbolift section 13 Alpha (D7). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Turbolift
- **Jefferies tubes:** maintenance crawlways; emergency movement when turbolifts fail; sealable; used for fitness drills (VOY "Learning Curve"). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Jefferies_tube
- **Force fields:** rated **levels 1–10** (10 strongest); block matter and (generally) transporter beams; level 10 can cut a Borg drone from the Collective; seal hull breaches. **Failure:** Borg adapt/walk through standard fields. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Force_field
- **Airlocks:** ingress/egress points, typically port/starboard. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Airlock
- **Escape pods:** auxiliary craft for abandoning ship; D1 has escape pods per Star Trek: The Magazine **[uncertain]**. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Escape_pod
- **Cargo bays:** general storage; **Cargo Bay 2** has **adjustable environmental controls for organic storage** (VOY "Parallax"); one retrofitted to airponics. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Cargo_bay

## 11. Airponics bay & kitchen (D11)
Cargo bay retrofitted as an aeroponics/hydroponics garden (not fitted at launch); created/maintained by Kes to **reduce replicator energy drain**; continued after she left (Crewman Bronowski); Neelix ran the kitchen (VOY "Parallax", "Cold Fire"). https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Airponics_bay

## 12. Shuttlebay & shuttlecraft
**Shuttlebay 1** (D10, aft dorsal, L-shaped, arresting field) + larger **Shuttlebay 2** aft for construction/repair, depressurizable, launch-capable. Standard: Class 2, Type 6, Type 8 shuttles, plus a runabout-size **aeroshuttle** integrated under the hull. Variable-gravity; force fields can replace open space doors. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_class ; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Shuttlebay
**Delta Flyer** (2375, a 2377 second followed): larger than standard; Starfleet/Borg hybrid — tetraburnium alloy hull, retractable warp nacelles, parametallic plating, unimatrix shielding, Borg-inspired weapons incl. photonic missiles; multiple command stations; designed by Torres/Paris/Tuvok/Kim/Seven. Shuttles used when transporters fail or can't penetrate atmosphere. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Delta_Flyer_(2375)

## 13. Crew complement
Launched with **141** (VOY "Caretaker"); Janeway says "started with 153 crew"; rose to **152** after Maquis/Neelix/Kes; ~**150** through 2374–2377; operable with as few as **100** (VOY "The 37's"); capacity ~160. Initial crossing cost 12+ dead incl. first officer, chief engineer, entire medical staff, transporter chief. https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Voyager ; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Voyager_personnel

## What a simulation must model
1. **Deck/compartment graph**: 15 decks, named spaces as above.
2. **Crew records**: ~141–152 roster (rank/species/quarters deck), attrition on hull-breach/away events.
3. **Life support**: per-deck atmosphere + gravity-plating state; env-control node D12; hull-breach/force-field sealing.
4. **Inertial dampers**: state gating atmospheric entry and warp.
5. **Power budget**: three independent sources (holodeck reactors, shuttles, life support) + main grid; holodeck-reactor incompatibility; replicator rationing tied to reserves; gel-pack-grid isolation disables replicators.
6. **Transporters**: per-room pads; 40,000 km range / 10 km emergency; buffer occupancy + "unblockable once buffered"; biofilter; block list; site-to-site to sickbay.
7. **Replicators**: energy-ration accounting (object complexity), recycle-in, forward-gel-pack dependency.
8. **Holodecks**: separate-reactor emitters, molecular matter, cascade-failure incompatibility.
9. **Medical**: 3 biobeds + 1 surgical bed; EMH availability; mobile-emitter detached state; medication via replicator.
10. **Movement**: power-gated turbolifts + sealable Jefferies tubes; escape pods; airlocks; shuttlebay launch/recovery with force-field doors.
11. **Computer integrity**: gel-pack health (infection/radiation/burnout); spare-pack inventory.
12. **Auxiliary craft**: shuttle roster (Class 2/6/8, aeroshuttle, Delta Flyer) as expendable, spares-hungry assets.
