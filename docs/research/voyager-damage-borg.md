## 1. Damage and attrition

### 1.1 Year of Hell — primary case study
Annorax's Krenim temporal weapon rewrites history, each incursion hitting Voyager with a spacetime shock wave; its chroniton torpedoes pass through shields for sitting slightly out of phase with space-time **[YOHT][YOH1]**.

Cumulative damage by day:
- **Day 1:** 15 wounded, main power down, environmental control dead on Decks 7–8, main computer core offline **[YOH1]**.
- **Day 32:** EPS conduits across Deck 5, sections 10–53 "obliterated"; two crew (Emmanuel and another) die **[YOH1]**. Eleven photon torpedoes remained **[YOH1]**.
- **Day 47:** nineteen main power relays severed, disabling the turbolift network **[YOHT]**. An undetonated chroniton torpedo in a Deck 11 Jefferies tube detonates, **permanently blinding Tuvok** within that timeline **[YOH1]**.
- **Day 65:** Deck 11 power grid destroyed; replicators badly damaged → emergency rations; environmental control failing, **seven decks uninhabitable**; crew in bunks; bridge "totally wrecked"; astrometrics offline **[YOH1]**.
- **Day 70:** escape at warp 7 with structural integrity failed — "large, jagged pieces of the outer hull peel off and break away" **[YOH1]**. Paris's **transverse bulkheads** (inspired by the *Titanic*) seal sections **[YOH1]**. Janeway orders abandon ship; escape pods carry most of the crew **[YOH1]**.
- **Day 226–257:** port saucer largely gone; bridge all but destroyed; six photon torpedoes left; a friendly ship collides with the primary hull, destroying "a huge chunk of the saucer" and leaving only a fritzy emergency force field between Janeway and space; Janeway rams the weapon ship and **Voyager is destroyed** **[YOH2]**.

**Never restored within the timeline:** dead crew, Tuvok's sight, the destroyed decks. The arc is wiped only because destroying the weapon ship erases its own history changes, leaving Voyager undamaged at Day 1 **[YOH2]** — the reset button *Long Way Home* rejects: canon attrition heals only via an external timeline event.

The earlier "Before and After" iteration **[YOHT]**: torpedoes penetrate the hull, breaching Deck 7; the engineering station explodes, **killing Janeway and Torres** plus nine others; power down Decks 4–12; the Doctor offline; leaked chroniton radiation irradiates most of the crew.

### 1.2 Hull breaches and decompression
Explosive decompression is a violent pressure drop within one tenth of a second, caused by containment-field failure at an airlock/hatch or a major hull breach **[DEC]**.

### 1.3 Warp core, containment, structural integrity
A warp core breach is antimatter-containment loss whose contact with ship matter destroys the vessel; it is "almost always irreversible" once safety systems fail **[WCB]**. Core ejection is a last-ditch, "often unreliable" response **[WCE]**. A Kazon torpedo blew out Voyager's containment generator in 2372, preventing warp **[WCB]**; the Caretaker's displacement wave caused microfractures in her warp core **[INT]**. Voyager ejected her core in 2371 (dark-matter nebula) and 2374 (tachyon overload), the second recovered from the Caatati **[WCE]**.

### 1.4 Red alert / emergency protocol
Red alert is the highest alert status — weapons and shields to full — triggered by attack, boarding, decompression, imminent core breach, or evacuation. Raising shields triggers it automatically; Seven of Nine could initiate it **[RED]**.

### 1.5 Condition at series' end
Voyager is **not destroyed** in the prime timeline: she reaches Earth in 2378, and by 2402 is "Preserved, Fleet Museum, Athan Prime" **[VOY]**. Baseline: **15 decks, 343 m, ~160 crew, 700,000 t, sustainable warp 9.975** **[VOY]**.

## 2. Borg boarding and assimilation

### 2.1 How boarding happens
Engaging a ship, the Borg lock a tractor beam, defeat shields, then either extract sections with a **cutting beam**, **transport drones to key areas**, or both **[ASI]**. A transported boarding party captured Picard **[FB]**. A subdued ship is stripped for parts and, if possible, towed into a Borg hangar **[ASI]**. Drones ignore intruders on their own ships "as long as the intruders did not interfere" **[BORG]**.

### 2.2 Drones
Drones are assimilated individuals with no individuality; implants are chosen by function, linked to the hive by a homing device with a unique translink frequency via subspace transceivers **[DRONE]**. Each drone's **assimilation tubules** "penetrate most known materials" and can assimilate "a single individual, or an entire starship, replicating components to interface with local technology" **[DRONE]**. Personal shields adapt to energy weapons, shared Collective-wide; exo-plating withstands a five-million-gigawatt surge but not projectiles **[DRONE]**. Drones must regenerate in alcoves (~200 hours max); absent one, a Federation power conduit at the right frequency can feed a drone **[DRONE]**. Designations ("Three of Nine") encode a drone's position within a unimatrix **[DRONE]**.

### 2.3 The assimilation process
Tubules inject nanoprobes into the bloodstream the moment a drone is within arm's reach; nanoprobes attach to red blood cells and circulate **[ASI]**. Stages: (1) injection; (2) skin mottles; (3) rudimentary implants form and the victim links to the hive (life signs turn erratic); (4) surgical implants, limb replacement, eyepieces, exo-plating; (5) a new drone, redesignated, whose life signs no longer read as its species **[ASI]**. A neural transceiver lets the Collective usurp mind and body **[ASI]**. Assimilation takes hours for a newcomer but **seconds for a former drone** **[ASI]**.

### 2.4 Reversibility
Reversal is possible only in narrow windows. The "most basic and arguably most practical remedy" is to euthanize the victim before nanoprobes install standard defenses **[ASI]**. Picard was isolated from the Collective by Data and recovered after the cube's destruction, though trauma was severe and Borg alterations lingered decades **[ASI]**. Seven of Nine, assimilated at six, spent 24 years in the Collective and was liberated in 2374 — never fully restored **[S7]**. A neural suppressant (2377) let Janeway, Tuvok and Torres be physically assimilated without losing individuality; implants were then removed **[ASI]**. The 80,000 drones severed in the Nekrit Expanse removed "most, but not all" implants and formed the Cooperative **[ASI][UNI]**.

### 2.5 Collective and vinculum
The Collective is a subspace-linked hive mind governed by the Queen, its only fully autonomous member **[COLL]**. A link can be severed by Queen termination, natural phenomena, physical damage, security/dampening fields, or submatrix collapse; a disconnected drone is an "xB," and severed drones still carry "secondary objectives" for a long time **[COLL]**. Every Borg vessel has a **vinculum** interconnecting its drones and purging individuality; a central plexus links ships **[BORG][VINC]**. In 2375 Species 6339 infected a cube's vinculum: drones could no longer suppress assimilated personalities, and the cube was destroyed within three days — the vinculum survived and infected Seven **[VINC]**. Borg ships regenerate from damage but self-destruct when critically compromised **[BORG]**.

### 2.6 Adaptation
The Borg adapt "within minutes": shields normally nullify nearly any energy weapon and weapons penetrate nearly any shield **[BORG]**, by the whole Collective computing all responses to a threat at once **[COLL]**.

### 2.7 Countermeasures
- **Force/dampening fields** sever the Collective link **[COLL]**.
- **Explosive decompression:** Chakotay decompressed Cargo Bay 2 to kill the Borg; only Seven survived, clinging to a Jefferies-tube ridge **[DEC]**.
- **Weapon remodulation:** the bio-molecular warhead — a photon torpedo delivering Borg nanoprobes — was built by Voyager with the Borg against Species 8472 **[BMW][NANO]**.
- **Holograms:** *uncertain* — no Memory Alpha source states the Borg ignore holograms.

## 3. Canon examples of a ship/crew lost or permanently changed
- **Voyager destroyed** in the Year of Hell timeline by ramming the weapon ship; Janeway dead in "Before and After" **[YOHT][YOH2]**.
- **Duplicate Voyager** destroyed in "Deadlock"; Harry Kim and Naomi's newborn lost **[VOY]**.
- **Silver Blood Voyager** (a copy) destroyed in 2375, ejecting its core first **[WCE]**.
- **Crew permanently altered:** Seven of Nine **[S7]**; Tuvok blinded within the deleted timeline **[YOH1]**.
- **80,000 severed drones** forming the Cooperative **[ASI][UNI]**; Species 6339's cube destroyed by vinculum pathogen **[VINC]**.

Prime-timeline Voyager always resets by episode's end; **canon has no starship permanently degraded across a series** — the gap *Long Way Home* fills.

## 4. What a simulation must model

### 4.1 Damage / attrition
**State:** per-deck hull integrity/pressure and life support; per-deck EPS power; replicator/material stock; crew roster (able/wounded/dead); torpedo and phaser stocks; shields; structural integrity; warp capability; subsystem flags (turbolift, computer core, sensors, sickbay, astrometrics). **Progression:** non-regenerating damage; cascading failures (relay loss → turbolift/lighting; overload → deck explosion **[YOH1]**); repair consuming scarce materials and time; breach → bulkhead seal or decompression. **Threshold:** uninhabitable-deck and casualty counts drive a rising "sustainability" curve until abandon-ship is the only rational call (Chakotay's Day 32 proposal; Janeway's Day 73 concession) **[YOH1]**. **No reset:** damage accumulates across the whole arc (canon erases it only via the forbidden timeline event **[YOH2]**).

### 4.2 Borg boarding
**State:** drones aboard; crew assimilated; vinculum/link integrity; per-weapon adaptation level; force/dampening fields; per-crewmember nanoprobe stage (1–5); local technology assimilated. **Progression:** boarding (transporter or cutting beam) → drones spread and assimilate → each assimilation adds a drone *and* advances adaptation → severed drones still execute secondary objectives **[COLL]** → drones reaching the vessel's processing core restructure ship systems **[DRONE]**. **Threshold:** a **time-to-organize** timer starts the moment drones are aboard unopposed — the "left alone long enough" the owner wants. Below it, crew purge via decompression, force fields, or remodulation **[DEC][COLL]**; above it the flip is irreversible (stage 5, or Borg self-destruct **[BORG]**). Model a short per-person reversibility window (nanoprobes counterable only in the first minutes **[ASI]**).
