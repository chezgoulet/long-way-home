# USS Voyager — Propulsion & Power (canon research)

Sources: Memory Alpha (MA) and Memory Beta (MB). Every claim carries its URL.
Confidence notes inline. "Not stated" = the source does not say.

---

## 1. Matter/antimatter reaction assembly (warp core)

- **What it does:** The M/ARA is the main energy reactor; deuterium and antideuterium are injected in an *intermix ratio* into a dilithium-moderated reaction, mutually annihilating to release energy. The dilithium crystal/articulation frame regulates the reaction. On Federation ships it is the warp core. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Matter-antimatter_reaction_assembly)
- **Canon specifics:** Voyager's core is a **class 9 warp drive with a tricyclic input manifold**, providing a **maximum output of four thousand teradynes per second** (4000 TD/s). (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Voyager ; MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Warp_core — both cite VOY "Drone"/"Relativity")
- **Flow:** electro-plasma exits in twin power-transfer conduits to the nacelles; EPS taps bleed power for other systems. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Warp_core)
- **Location:** main engineering is on **Deck 11**, built around the vertical core; a single-person elevator/catwalk gives access. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_class ; VOY "Caretaker")
- **Limits/failure modes:** coolant leak → runaway reaction → antimatter containment loss → **warp core breach** (irreversible; destroys the ship). Microfractures, magnetic-constrictor lock-down, and injector faults are recurring. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Warp_core_breach)
- **On failure:** the core can be **ejected** (see §6); without it the ship runs on **impulse power and batteries** only — no warp. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Warp_core_breach)
- **Evidence:** VOY "Caretaker" (microfracture, near-breach); "Threshold"; "Day of Honor"; "Renaissance Man"; "Cathexis".

## 2. Warp nacelles & variable-geometry pylons

- **What they do:** Warp coils in the nacelles create the subspace field; large conduits run warp core → nacelle struts → nacelles, driven by plasma injectors. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Electro-plasma_system)
- **Variable-geometry pylon:** introduced ~2371; **Intrepid-class only** canon class. Nacelles **raise to warp position** and lower to a streamlined sublight position. Pylons raise **any time the warp field is above idle**, even without propulsive effect. Design goal: improve engine efficiency / optimise field stress on long warp-8+ cruises. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Variable_geometry_pylon ; VOY "Learning Curve")
- **Impulse placement:** Voyager's **main impulse engines sit on the aft end of the pylons** leading to the warp nacelles; Intrepid also has **auxiliary impulse reactors**. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_class ; VOY "Phage")
- **Confidence:** the pylon "environmental protection" benefit (no subspace damage) is *background/speculation* in MA and contradicted by VOY "Renaissance Man" — treat as soft. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Variable_geometry_pylon)

## 3. Warp speed scales & Voyager's speeds

- **24th-c (TNG) scale:** `speed = wf^(10/3) × c`, infinite at warp 10; high sub-10 speeds use decimals (e.g. warp 9.975). (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Warp_factor)
- **Voyager top sustainable:** **warp 9.975** (stated max cruising speed, ~**3,053 c** per Voyager Technical Manual); it could **sustain over warp 9.95 only a couple of hours**. Normal travel ~**warp 6.2**; **warp 6** is the class cruising speed. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Voyager ; "Threshold", "Distant Origin", "Pathfinder")
- **Structural limit:** at ~warp 9.97 the computer warned of **imminent structural collapse**. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Voyager — "Threshold")
- **Journey math:** ~75,000 ly home; ~1,000 c effective projected rate. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Maximum_warp)
- **Intrepid maxspeed** = warp 9.975. (MB: https://memory-beta.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_class)

## 4. Impulse engines

- **What they do:** essentially an **augmented fusion rocket** — fusion reactor(s) + driver-coil assembly + vectored thrust nozzle. Fusion plasma can propel, or be routed via **EPS** to power other systems. Driver coils generate a subspace field improving thrust. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Impulse_engine)
- **Fuel:** **deuterium-based fusion**; also serves as the ship's **secondary power system**. (MB: https://memory-beta.fandom.com/wiki/Impulse_engine)
- **Sublight role:** impulse covers sublight + low warp; by the 2270s impulse alone could reach warp 0.5. Not stated for Voyager (Intrepid likely similar). (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Impulse_engine)
- **Failure:** loss of impulse leaves batteries/emergency power; combat damage to impulse/pylons is a recurring VOY plot lever.

## 5. EPS (electro-plasma system) & power distribution

- **What it does:** the **primary energy-distribution grid** — stores energy as high-energy plasma and routes it via **EPS conduits / plasma grid** from the core to all systems. Large conduits feed nacelles; **EPS taps** convert to conventional electricity at subsystems; some use **plasma manifolds** to manage conversion. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Electro-plasma_system)
- **Failure modes:** conduit ruptures, **plasma-coolant leaks** (toxic, can force abandon-ship), relay/conduit blowouts causing widespread outages across sensors, comms, tactical, environmental control. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Electro-plasma_system ; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Plasma_manifold_system)
- **Voyager specifics:** a plasma manifold destabilised almost to breach (VOY "Prime Factors"); isomagnetic EPS conduits added to the Delta Flyer manifold (VOY "Extreme Risk"); MB notes some decks' **power relays used Borg tech** and **EPS + backup adopted Enaran power-conservation tech**. (MB: https://memory-beta.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Voyager)

## 6. Antimatter storage, deuterium, core ejection

- **Deuterium:** heavy-hydrogen isotope; deuterium + tritium mix fuels the reaction. Stored as **cryogenic liquid** in **deuterium tanks**, connected to the deuterium-injection subsystem feeding the dilithium chamber. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Deuterium ; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Deuterium_tank)
- **Voyager's tank risk:** bio-neural gel-pack false readings reported **deuterium tank breach cascading into antimatter storage**; Doctor advised ejecting tanks (VOY "One"). (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Deuterium_tank)
- **Scarcity:** Voyager repeatedly faced **deuterium shortages** and had to source it locally; when the core is offline, **deuterium still feeds auxiliary fusion reactors and impulse drive**. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Deuterium ; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Warp_core — VOY "The Cloud", "Demon", "Bliss", "The Void")
- **Antimatter:** contained by **magnetic fields**; antimatter pods/tanks hold it; containment loss = breach. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Antimatter ; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Warp_core)
- **Ejection:** requires the **chief engineer's / senior staff authorization code** (voice: "prepare to eject the warp core, authorization [code]" → "warp ejection systems enabled" → "eject the warp core"). Core exits through the **ejection port on the underside of the secondary hull**, launched by **magnetic rails**. Ship then flees under impulse. A non-critical core can be **recovered by tractor beam**. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Intrepid_class ; https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Warp_core_ejection_system ; VOY "Cathexis", "Day of Honor", "Course: Oblivion", "Renaissance Man")

## 7. What "power" means operationally

- **Independent power sources aboard Voyager: shuttlecraft, life support, and the holodecks** — the three systems with their own power, separate from the main grid. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Voyager — VOY "Macrocosm")
- **Priority when core is lost:** impulse/auxiliary fusion reactors + **batteries** sustain the ship; **warp requires the core + nacelles**. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/Warp_core_breach)
- **What browns out first (inferred, MEDIUM confidence):** replicators/holodecks are the classic first thing rationed (VOY conservation: airponics replaced replicator use; VOY "Parallax"/"Phage"). Not stated in a strict priority table — the sources give no canonical brownout order.
- **Recovery lever:** surplus power reserve enabled replicating alloys/spare parts. (MA: https://memory-alpha.fandom.com/wiki/USS_Voyager)

## What a simulation must model (minimum state variables & behaviours)

**Core loop**
- `warp_core`: online/offline, output 0–4000 TD/s, health (microfracture/coolant leak/constrictor state), breach_risk, ejected(bool).
- `eps_grid`: per-sector conduit health (decks), tap load, plasma-coolant integrity. Rupture → localised outage; coolant leak → crew hazard.
- `antimatter`: containment %, pod integrity (magnetic-field failure = instant breach), fuel stock.
- `deuterium`: tank level (cryogenic), tank integrity, collected-vs-consumed (scarcity is a Voyager theme); feeds core injection *and* aux fusion reactors.
- `nacelles`: deployed/stowed (pylons auto-raise when warp field > idle), warp-coil health, warp-field stress.
- `warp_speed`: 0 → 9.975 sustained ceiling; **>9.95 for ~2h triggers engine/structural damage**; hard wall near 9.97 structural collapse; cruise default 6.2.
- `impulse`: fused, independent of core once running; provides sublight + emergency power; on aft pylons (shared damage location).
- `power_sources`: main grid, impulse/fusion, batteries, + **three independent sources (shuttlecraft, life support, holodecks)**.
- `power_budget`: EPS allocation priority list; brownout order (replicators/holodecks → non-critical → sensors → …).

**Behaviours that make it *feel* right**
- Damage timidly cascades: pylon/impulse hit or conduit rupture → localised EPS outage → system trips; coolant leak → breach countdown.
- Core loss = no warp, running on impulse + batteries; every warp action gated by core+containment.
- Ejection is a last-ditch, authorized action with a real chance of failure; recovering the core is possible.
- Fuel is finite and directional (deuterium scarcity), and manifolds/conduits are the fragile middle layer between reactor and everything else.
