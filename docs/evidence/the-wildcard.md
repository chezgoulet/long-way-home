# Evidence: the wildcard attempt

**Session 2026-11-08.** Stage E's deferred item, `docs/endings.md`, "Structure": *"A wildcard is
attemptable. The player can make a play for somewhere that has not invited them, and fail to reach it
or be refused at it. … the wildcard is the one place in the endgame where something is spent that
cannot be got back, on a hope."* The acceptance line is: *"A wildcard attempt can fail, and the
failure is attributable to what the player did."*

## What was built

- **The play is a place not in the accrued set.** `SolveWildcard(s, d, reachRoll, receiveRoll)` is pure
  and returns the whole outcome. It is offered for the Empire, a settlement, the Collective and a sale,
  and only when there is a shuttle in the bay, the deuterium reserve is above the tenth a play takes,
  the crystal is not spent, and — for a sale — a trader has been met. A place the run has already
  earned is not a play, and an unoffered attempt spends nothing.
- **The spend cannot be got back.** `AttemptWildcard` spends **a shuttle out of the bay, a tenth of the
  deuterium reserve and a twentieth of the crystal** before the draw
  (`WILDCARD_DEUTERIUM = 0.10`, `WILDCARD_CRYSTAL = 0.05`, ours and recorded). The spend is the saved
  stores and the shuttle record, so a save taken before the play replays it; the draws use
  `s.riskRolls`, the same saved deterministic counter `UseSystem` and `AttemptRising` draw on, so the
  outcome also replays.
- **Two failures, each attributable.**
  - **Not reached** is a read of the ship's own condition: the warp drive, the hull, the reserve, and
    the play's odds, named in the fact.
  - **Refused at it** is a read of the record: the reader's own first evidence line is the reason, and
    `ReadEnding(s, d, true, false)` returns that reader's refusal rather than a fallback to another
    destination.
  A play that does not reach still ends the run at Earth, which is always open; the failure stays in
  the log and the record is still the last frame.
- **The odds are reads, not a stored score.** Reach is a weighted read of the warp drive, the hull, the
  reserve and the best flight hand; reception is the design's own lens for that reader — funerals,
  promises and lies for the Empire; promises and the people who would stay for the settlement; 1.0 for
  the Collective, which takes what comes to it; the hull for a buyer.
- **Where it is reached.** `ship wildcard <destination> [conduit]` in `module/ship/g_ship.cpp`, and the
  endgame screen offers the play on a row that is not earned (`module/ui/ui_lwh_ending.cpp`). The
  screen sends the same command, so the console and the screen cannot diverge.

## The command

```sh
scripts/ending-check.sh
```

It compiles the ship tests on the host, runs the suite, prints `test_ship_core --endings`, then
falsifies five ways. The excerpt below is this session's run.

```text
== the wildcard
  WILDCARD: a play for The Klingon Empire; reach odds 31%, reception 50%
  NOT REACHED: the ship did not reach The Klingon Empire: the warp drive was at 20%, the hull at 30% and the reserve at 20%; the play's odds were 31%
  REFUSED AT IT: The Klingon Empire read the record and did not receive the ship: deaths recorded: 0
  SPENT: deuterium 100% -> 90%; crystal 100% -> 95%; shuttles lost 0 -> 1
  WILDCARD NEUTRAL: 0
  EARNED ALREADY: offered 0; the reserve is unchanged 1
```

The output is generated by `TestEndingWildcard` and the `--endings` print. The two failures are forced
with explicit draws (`SolveWildcard`'s `reachRoll` and `receiveRoll`), so the test does not depend on a
random outcome; the attempt's own draw is the saved counter and is not asserted to a particular side.

## PASS lines, and what each is evidence of

- **PASS — a place not invited has a play.** `WILDCARD: a play for The Klingon Empire; reach odds 31%,
  reception 50%` is `SolveWildcard` on a damaged ship for a destination `DestinationAvailable` refuses.
  `TestEndingWildcard` asserts `probe.offered` and that the target is not in the accrued set.
- **PASS — not reaching is attributable to the ship's state.** `NOT REACHED: … the warp drive was at
  20%, the hull at 30% and the reserve at 20%; the play's odds were 31%`. The test asserts the failure
  names the warp drive and the hull, and that the same string passes `EndingToneFindings`.
- **PASS — being refused is attributable to the record.** `REFUSED AT IT: The Klingon Empire read the
  record and did not receive the ship: deaths recorded: 0` is the reader's own evidence line, produced
  by `ReadEnding(s, target, true, false)`. The test asserts the line names the deaths.
- **PASS — the play spends what cannot be got back.** `SPENT: deuterium 100% -> 90%; crystal 100% ->
  95%; shuttles lost 0 -> 1`. `AttemptWildcard` mutates the stores and marks one in-bay shuttle
  `SHUTTLE_LOST` before the draw; the test asserts both stores fall and the log carries the spend.
- **PASS — an earned place is not a play.** `EARNED ALREADY: offered 0; the reserve is unchanged 1` is
  `AttemptWildcard` on the earned run: it refuses, takes no draw and spends nothing.
- **PASS — the wildcard's own words are neutral.** `WILDCARD NEUTRAL: 0` scans the three produced
  lines with `EndingToneFindings`.

## Decisions taken where the document leaves a choice

- **The cost is a shuttle, 10% of the deuterium and 5% of the crystal.** Recorded as constants in the
  header; this is ours, and is the "something spent that cannot be got back" the design asks for.
- **The play is offered only for the four places with a door to knock on.** Earth is always open, and
  "no reader" has nobody to make a play to.
- **The odds are reads of the same state play already writes.** No reputation store was added.
- **A play that never reaches ends the run at Earth.** Earth is always reachable, the failure remains
  in the log, and the reading that follows is Earth's — not a special "you failed" screen.
- **The spend and the draw are existing saved state.** No new field, so `SAVE_VERSION` remains **66**.

## What could not be run here, and is not claimed

- **Not watched in a graphical session.** The wildcard is reached by the console and by the endgame
  screen; the screen's rendered frame is not claimed here.
- **The odds' calibration is a judgement, not a measurement.** The weights are ours, small and in the
  source so the owner can overrule them; no play session has tuned them.
