# Evidence: the deck-12 readout — the deck's air drawn where the problem is

Walkthrough **G18** (`docs/walkthrough.md`): *"The air/endurance countdown is not drawn where the
problem is. To close: an environmental-control readout at the deck-12 watch console."* This is that
readout: the deck's own air, the countdown to what the design calls unbreathable, and which source
is holding that air, on a surface at the watch console in the deck 12 room.

Built on `feat/the-deck12-readout`, cut from `testing`, 2026-10-11. **No new mechanism**: the air
model, the three sources, the clocks and the per-deck feed are the air pass's (`docs/evidence/the-air.md`,
`docs/damage-and-budgets.md`); this pass draws them. No console verb, no HUD tick, no scenario
content, no engine change, no new art, and **no save change** — the readout is a drawing of state
that already exists and already survives a save.

Every figure below is the output of the command named beside it, on this tree, with the merged ship
built (`scripts/build-ship.sh`) and the module and engine built. Nothing is asserted without the
command that produced it.

## What was built

- **A surface of its own at the watch console.** The deck-12 re-dress already stands a watch console
  in the room (copied from `tour/deck11`, `docs/evidence/deck12-redress.md`), with the life-support
  station marker `lwh_station_0` in front of it. On its face toward the watch post,
  `tools/shipmap/dressdeck12.py` now builds a **128 wide × 64 tall × 8 thick** screen: the readout.
  It carries a new material, `lwh/env`, and nothing else changed in the room.
- **A second live surface, and why.** `lwh/env` is a shader alias beside the status panel's
  `lwh/panel` (`tools/shipmap/data/lwh_panel.shader`), with a placeholder image at its own size
  (`gfx/lwh/env.tga`, 128×64, generated by `tools/shipmap/panelart.py`). The status panel is drawn
  wherever it appears — it is the ship's panel, and it says ship-wide things. The finding is about
  *this deck*, so the deck's air gets its own image and its own surface. The engine capability is
  the one the status panel already used (`gi.UpdatePanelImage`, `patches/0014`); the module draws
  the readout at the same 5 Hz (`module/ship/lwh_panel.cpp`, `RenderEnv`).
- **What it says.** Four short lines, from the same functions the simulation and the console read,
  so the picture and the number cannot disagree:

  | line | source of the value |
  |---|---|
  | `DECK 12 AIR` | the room's deck (`ENV_DECK = 12`, `docs/ship-master-map.md`) |
  | `AIR n%` | `Deck::atmosphere`, as a percentage |
  | `HOLDING` / `n.n MIN TO AIRLESS` / `AIRLESS` | `MinutesOfAir(s, 12)`: `-1` is holding, a finite number is the countdown to `AIRLESS` |
  | `SOURCE MAIN GRID` / `... AUX FUSION` / ... | `AirSourceName(AirSourceOf(s, 12))` — the feed the air pass built (grid, impulse, aux fusion, batteries) |

## The defect this pass found and fixed: q3map2 could not *size* the live surfaces

The readout's face is one tile of its 128×64 image at texture scale 1. It was not, at first: the
picture came out **split and repeated, twice across the face**, and a test pattern showed why.

```
    before:  the frame reads   green | red | blue | green | red | blue | white
    after:   the frame reads   green | red | blue | white
```

Cause: the shaders `lwh/panel` and `lwh/env` and their images were in the **pak** only. q3map2
compiles the map and sizes a face from the image; unable to find either, it fell back to a 64×64
default, so a 128-unit face was baked as **two tiles** (the BSP's own texture coordinates for the
`+x` face run `s` from `-1.00` to `+1.00` over 128 units — two tiles, read out of the compiled
`.bsp`). The fix is in `scripts/build-ship.sh`: the compiler's own game dir
(`build/lwhshaders/`) now carries the same shader and the same placeholder images, so q3map2 sizes
them (128×64) and the face is one tile. **This also corrects every existing `lwh/panel` surface**,
which had been baked the same way — the status panels on the generated decks were two tiles in each
axis. Both the status panel's check (`scripts/s4-panel-check.sh`) and the room checks are unchanged
and still pass.

## The falsification: the wiring cut, and the check catching it

frozen state whatever the deck does. Run through the check with `LWH_ENV_BREAK=1`:


## What could not be verified

- **Whether it reads as the ship telling you something** rather than as a number in a box. That is
  the owner's eye, and it is not answered by any transcript. What is reported is what the frames
  show and the numbers above; the judgement is his.
- **Whether the console's face is the right place for it** rather than the wall beside it, or above
  the plant. The finding says "at the deck-12 watch console", and this is that console's face; a
  different surface is a different pass.
- **The room around the post is dim** (`median 12–30` on four of six vantages). It is the vantage
  facing the console, and the place measures legible from its own arrival and from two of the six
  vantages; but a person who wants a lit watch station may disagree, and that call is recorded
  rather than hidden.

## Judgement calls, named as calls

1. **A second live surface, not lines on the status panel.** The status panel is drawn wherever it
   appears, so its lines are the ship's, not a deck's; the finding is about deck 12. `lwh/env` is a
   second image and a second shader, using the engine capability that already existed. Cost: the
   ship now draws two panels per tick (negligible).
2. **The readout is deck 12's own air** — the deck the console is on — not the ship's worst deck.
   The finding says "the deck's air"; the worst-deck reading would be a different surface.
3. **The screen is a whole tile of the image.** 128×64 at texture scale 1 on a 128×64 face; the
   texture shift is reduced to its phase (`tools/shipmap/dressdeck12.py`, `readout_screen`), because
   a picture is not a tiling material.
4. **The post is 112 units off the face, stepped back to 144** where the room is not clear. At the
   life-support station marker (`lwh_station_0`, 32 units from the console) the console fills the
   frame; the readout needs a standing step back.
5. **No light was added** in the room (`the dark-room rule`, above). The place reads; the dark
   vantages face the console.
6. **The falsification freezes the readout** rather than dimming it, so the check's *difference*
   assertion is what catches the break — the assertion the finding is about.
7. **The compiler-side fix (`build/lwhshaders/`) is part of this pass**, because the readout cannot
   be drawn without it; it also corrects the existing status panels, and neither the panel's check
   nor any room check changed result.
8. **The readout uses the short source names** (`MAIN GRID`, `AUX FUSION`, ...) rather than
   `AirSourceName`'s prose, because 128 pixels across is 21 characters; the mapping is local to the
   panel (`ShortSource`, `module/ship/lwh_panel.cpp`) and the full names are unchanged in the
   console and the log.
