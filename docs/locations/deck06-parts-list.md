# Deck 6 — Holodeck 2, Armory and Quarters: parts list

The brief's section 4 leaves the parts list open, "to be completed from the pak census (holodeck door
frames, armory lockers)". This is that list. Deck 6 is the one **composition**: three zones from three
maps, so this census is over all three sources, taken by `tools/shipmap/dressdeck06.py --census`,
plus the pieces the composition adds on top of them, each checked against the shipped game.

## The three sources, and what each zone contributes

| zone | source (published map) | what it is | what the copy takes |
|---|---|---|---|
| quarters frontage | `tour/deck09` | the crew-quarters deck | a 1,088 x 336 slice of its hall (x -3788..-2700, y -3300..-2964): the hall-and-alcoves frontage |
| armory | `_brig` | the game's only security interior (it carries the brig) | a 512 x 336 slice around the brig's own player start (x -320..192, y -460..-124): lockers, grating, lighting |
| holodeck arch | `Tour/_holodeck_firingrange` | a `_holodeck_*` programme map | the arch cluster (x 32..152, y -96..112), turned 90 degrees and placed twice |

dropped. No new models: the deck names nothing the shipped game does not have.

## The check

Every face texture used by the generated `deck06.map` resolves:

```
$ python3 tools/shipmap/parts_census.py --map build/ship/generated/deck06.map --game build/baseEF \
      --census build/ship/deck06-census.json --shader build/ship/files/scripts/lwh_detail06.shader
build/ship/generated/deck06.map: 37 distinct materials
  present as shipped images:      2694
  declared by shipped shaders:    2070
  our own aliases:                63
  in the reuse source census:     116
PASS  every material already exists in the game; no new art
```

The build runs this check, so a deck 6 that named art the game does not have would fail the build.
**No new art.** The command and its verdict are also in `docs/evidence/deck06-composition.md`.
